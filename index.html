Why join

A working community, not a mailing list

Four things you get the day you join — and keep getting.

A room that's always open

A working Slack where people post the half-formed thing before it is finished. Ask the question you can't ask at work yet, and get answers from people with no stake in your org chart.

Events, constantly

Live, online, free, and small enough to actually talk. A recurring hands-on hour plus bigger forum sessions that take one hard question at a time. No registration fee, no sales pitch at the end.

Research you helped write

197 learning professionals across six sectors told us which skills matter now and what is getting in the way. The report is free, the data is open, and the next round is built from your answers too.

Cross-sector on purpose

AI may be the first force in a generation that genuinely levels the playing field across sectors. The higher-ed answer and the corporate answer are more useful side by side than either is alone.
