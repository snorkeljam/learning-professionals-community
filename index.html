The learning profession has never had to change at this rate. No one person has the answer, so we're working it out together.

AI is the part everyone can name, and it is one piece of a much wider shift in how people learn. Across corporate learning, higher education, K-12, consulting, association management, workforce development and customer education, we are asking the same questions in separate rooms. This is the room where we ask them together.
