The Learning Professionals Community meets in person for the first time. We're bringing the profession into one room to work on what is changing in this field, and on the durable skills we carry through it.

Productive Tension

Two things are true at once. AI is changing how people learn and work faster than any of us planned for. And the skills that make us human, like judgment, care, curiosity and connection, are worth more than ever.

We think the most useful conversations happen in the space in between. We're looking for sessions that stay there.

What We're Looking For

Facilitators, speakers, experts and practitioners with a point of view that will move a room, and above all sessions that only work because the room is cross-sector.

Most of the day will be hands-on, so workshops are what we need most. We're still shaping the programme. Tell us what you would run and we'll work out where it fits.
